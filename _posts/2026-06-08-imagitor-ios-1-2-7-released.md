We're thrilled to announce that **Imagitor version 1.2.7** is now available on the App Store! 

![Imagitor iOS Big Update](/assets/blog/ios-promo.png)

This major update brings a host of highly requested features, improved stability, and a better overall experience for all iOS and iPad users. Here’s a look at what’s new in this release.

## What's New in 1.2.7

### 🪄 AI Background Removal
Say goodbye to complex masking! We've integrated our powerful **AI Background Remover** directly into the image editor. You can now instantly isolate subjects from photos with a single tap, making it easier than ever to create stunning collages, logos, and custom compositions.

### 👤 User Accounts & Cloud Syncing
You can now create an account! With our new **Firebase Authentication and Firestore integration**, your profile and data are secure. This lays the groundwork for seamless cross-device syncing and cloud backups.

### 💳 Local Bank Payments in Pakistan
We are making our premium features more accessible. Users in Pakistan can now purchase packages using local banking methods directly within the app.

### 🔄 Update Dialog
Never miss out on the latest tools. We’ve added a friendly in-app update dialog so you'll always be notified when a new version of Imagitor is ready.

### 🎨 Online Templates
Jumpstart your creativity! We've fully ported the extensive library of online templates and projects from Android, giving you instant access to thousands of ready-made designs right from your iPhone or iPad.

### 🛠️ UI Enhancements & Stability
- Fixed navigation bar height issues for a cleaner interface.
- Resolved several crashes and made significant stability improvements under the hood.

## Recent iOS Highlights

In case you missed our previous updates leading up to this release, iOS users now enjoy:
- **Cross-Platform Parity:** We've fully ported the beloved Android font selection, symbol picker, and SVGKit-based shapes view to iOS.
- **Enhanced Text Tools:** Your text view now supports text gradients, stroke gradients, vertical alignment, and line height adjustments with full Android interoperability.
- **Image Cropping & Stickers:** We've combined the sticker and image views into one powerful tool and introduced a native image cropping feature.
- **Advanced Color Selection:** Experience our ported gradient color picker, now using the native iOS UIColor picker for a seamless feel.
- **Project Management:** You can now save, store, and load your local projects with ease.
- **Native Undo/Redo System:** A comprehensive history manager integrated across the Studio UI.
- **iPad Optimization:** A completely refactored iPad experience with a SplitViewController sidebar, floating zoom overlays, and dynamic UI scaling.

We are constantly working to bring you the best mobile design experience possible. Update to version 1.2.7 today and try out the new Background Remover!
