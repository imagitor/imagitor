Big news: **Imagitor is now a video editor too** — and not just any video editor. This is the **first ever Urdu video editor**: a real, timeline-based editor with the same design-quality Urdu typography you already use for your posts and posters, now in motion.

That's why the app has a new name: **Imagitor - Urdu Design & Video**.

![Imagitor Urdu video editor with timeline and animated Urdu text overlay on a video](/assets/screenshot/imagitor-urdu-video-editor.jpg)

## A real video editor, built Urdu-first

Video editors have been around for years — but none of them treat Urdu text as a first-class citizen. Broken Nastaliq rendering, no proper right-to-left support, and no real Urdu fonts. Imagitor changes that. Everything our design editor does for Urdu — Nastaliq fonts, text effects, gradients, shadows, strokes — now works **on video**.

## What you can do

### 🎬 Full timeline editing
Edit like the pros: a live preview on top and a **multi-track timeline** below. Trim clips from either edge, split at the playhead, reorder, duplicate, and delete. Add **transitions** between scenes and apply **filters** to your footage.

### ✍️ Animated Urdu text overlays
Overlay **animated Urdu, Arabic, and Persian text** on any video — with the same Nastaliq fonts and text effects from the design editor. Bring text in and out with animations, and add **stickers and shapes** on top of your footage, just like a design canvas.

### 🤖 AI Slides
Don't want to start from scratch? **AI Slides** turns a text prompt into a **ready-made slideshow video**. Describe your topic, and Imagitor generates styled text slides you can edit, re-time, and export — perfect for quotes, announcements, and narrated stories.

### 🎵 Free background music
Pick a soundtrack from a **free background music library**, drop it on the timeline, and trim it to fit your video.

### 📱 Made for status, reels, and shorts
Edit in **9:16, 1:1, 16:9** and more, then export and share straight to **WhatsApp status, Instagram, and YouTube**. Your videos are saved right to your gallery.

## Design + Video, one app

The best part: your design skills carry over. The video editor uses the same text tools, stickers, and shapes you already know from Imagitor's design editor — so a poster you love can become a video you love, in the same app, for free.

## Get started today

The Video Editor is rolling out now on Android. Open Imagitor, tap **Video** on the home screen, and start editing.
