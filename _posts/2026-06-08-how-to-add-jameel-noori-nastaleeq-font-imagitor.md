When it comes to Urdu typography, nothing beats the classic elegance of **Jameel Noori Nastaleeq**. Whether you're designing poetry posts, greeting cards, or YouTube thumbnails, this font is an absolute must-have for any Urdu designer.

Instead of searching the web to download it manually, we've made it incredibly easy to get Jameel Noori Nastaleeq directly inside Imagitor using our in-app Font Library! Here is how to add it to your app in just a few taps.

### Step 1: Open the Font Library
You can access the downloadable fonts library from two different places in Imagitor:
- **From Settings:** Go to the Home screen, tap the **Settings** gear icon, and select the **Download Fonts** option.
- **From the Editor:** Open any project, add some text, open the **Font** tool, navigate to the **USER** tab, and tap the **Download Online Fonts** button.

### Step 2: Download the Font
1. Once you are in the Font Library, you will see a list of premium online fonts available for download.
2. Scroll through the list (or select the **Urdu** language filter at the top) until you find **V3 جمیل نوری نستعلیق**.
3. Tap the **Download** button next to the font. 
4. Imagitor will automatically fetch the font and securely install it onto your device. You can track the progress right on your screen.

### Step 3: Where to Find Your Downloaded Font
Once the download is complete, the font is automatically added to your local collection! 

To start using your newly downloaded Jameel Noori Nastaleeq:
1. Open any project and select your text layer.
2. Open the **Font** picker from the bottom menu.
3. Swipe across the top categories to find the **USER** tab.
4. Tap the **USER** tab, and your beautiful Jameel Noori Nastaleeq font will be waiting there for you to use!

*Note: All downloaded online fonts are saved locally to your device, meaning you only have to download them once. They will always be available offline in your **USER** tab for all your future designs.*
